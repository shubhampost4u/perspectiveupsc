# 🚀 Google Play Store Publishing Guide

Complete step-by-step guide to publish Perspective UPSC app on Google Play Store.

---

## Prerequisites

- ✅ Google Play Developer Account ($25 one-time fee)
- ✅ Signed APK or AAB file (see BUILD_GUIDE.md)
- ✅ App Icon (512x512 PNG)
- ✅ Screenshots (at least 2 for phone)
- ✅ Feature Graphic (1024x500 PNG/JPG)
- ✅ Privacy Policy URL

---

## Step 1: Create Developer Account

### Register
1. Go to https://play.google.com/console
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Click **"Create account"**
4. Choose **"Organization"** or **"Individual"**
5. Accept **Developer Distribution Agreement**
6. Pay **$25 registration fee** (one-time, credit/debit card)
7. Complete **account details**:
   - Developer name: "Perspective UPSC" or your company name
   - Contact email: admin@perspectiveupsc.com
   - Phone number
   - Website: https://www.perspectiveupsc.com

### Verify Account
- Google may require additional verification
- Can take 24-48 hours for approval
- Check email for verification status

---

## Step 2: Create App

1. In Play Console, click **"Create app"**
2. Fill in details:

### App Details
- **App name:** Perspective UPSC
- **Default language:** English (United States)
- **App or game:** App
- **Free or paid:** Free

### Declarations
- ✅ I confirm this app complies with Google Play policies
- ✅ I acknowledge that this app has been reviewed
- ✅ I confirm this app is or will be exported compliant

3. Click **"Create app"**

## Step 9: Upload App

### Go to Production → Create New Release

1. Click **"Create new release"**
2. **Upload APK or AAB:**
   - Drag and drop your signed APK/AAB
   - Or click "Browse files"
   - **Recommended:** Upload AAB (smaller download for users)

3. **Release name:**
   - Auto-generated from versionName (e.g., "1.0.0 (1)")

4. **Release notes:**
```
Initial release of Perspective UPSC

✨ Features:
• Browse and purchase UPSC practice tests
• Take timed tests with real exam simulation
• View detailed results and performance analytics
• Cart with bundle discounts (10%, 15%, 25%)
• Secure payments via Razorpay
• Clean and intuitive interface
• Offline support for purchased tests

🎯 Perfect for UPSC Prelims, Mains, and Interview preparation!

For support: admin@perspectiveupsc.com
```

5. Click **"Save"** then **"Review release"**

## Updating the App

### Release New Version
1. Update `versionCode` and `versionName` in `build.gradle`
2. Build new signed APK/AAB
3. Go to **Production → Create new release**
4. Upload new APK/AAB
5. Add release notes:
```
Version 1.1.0 Update

🆕 New Features:
• Added dark mode support
• Improved test loading speed
• Enhanced analytics dashboard

🐛 Bug Fixes:
• Fixed payment confirmation issue
• Resolved offline mode crash
• Improved network error handling

🎨 Improvements:
• Better UI animations
• Faster app startup
• Reduced app size by 20%
```
6. Submit for review
7. Update typically approved faster (1-2 days)

## Common Issues

### App Not Showing in Search
- Wait 2-4 hours after approval
- Check in incognito mode
- Search exact package name initially
- May take days to rank for competitive keywords

### Low Download Numbers
- Improve ASO (title, description, keywords)
- Get initial reviews from friends/family
- Share on social media
- Run promotional campaigns
- Consider paid advertising

### Negative Reviews
- Respond professionally and quickly
- Acknowledge issues
- Mention if fix is in next update
- Ask to update review after fix
- Learn from feedback
